This app (Grand Prix, a text editor for Google Drive files) runs entirely in your browser. I do not run a server for it, and I do not receive, collect, or store any of your data. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary web server logs of requests to the site under its own policies.

When you sign in, the app asks Google for access to your Drive using these scopes: drive.file, drive.appdata, and drive.install. It uses that access to read the files you open with it, to save changes you make to those files or to files you create with it, and to keep one settings file (config.json) in the private application data folder that Google gives this app in your Drive. That settings file holds editor preferences such as theme, word wrap, whitespace rendering, and line numbers, so they follow you between devices. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

Your browser's local storage on your own machine holds a copy of those settings and the Google sign-in token the app receives, along with when it expires, so you do not have to sign in again on every page load. Nothing from Drive or from local storage is sent anywhere except to Google's APIs, and I do not share it with anyone. You can remove what the app has stored by clearing this site's data in your browser, by deleting config.json through the Drive settings for this app, and by revoking the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions or concerns can be sent as an issue at https://github.com/jkopczyn/grand-prix/issues.

This app has never been the subject of a warrant that would require it to give away information it collected. And probably never will be. It is likely that the app will be taken down if that ever happens, and only this page (with this paragraph removed) will remain up.
